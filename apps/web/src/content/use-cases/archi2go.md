Designed and built [Archi2go](https://archi2go.time2map.com) — a map for architects who travel. The React Native app (iOS & Android) and web companion keep a curated place collection synchronized with Mapbox: quiet cartography, progressive 3D landmarks, location-aware nearby places and shareable links that open the same story on the street or at the desk.
